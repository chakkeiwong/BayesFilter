## Intent and evidence contract

M13 has four states (two positions and two velocities), two range/bearing
observations per time, T=20, and two inferred log noise scales. The prior is
independent N(0, log(2)^2) on both scales, with support R^2. The remaining
coefficients and initial law are fixed by the existing fixture. The canonical
N=256 analytical force uses FP32, TF32, XLA, Contract E Cholesky reset and the
repository chunk policy (K=N). Saved-run FP32 discrepancies remain warnings.

Question: do the full canonical protection controls have a stable, finite
region on independent M13 calibration and validation datasets? The comparator
for each candidate is the next weaker protection in the existing geometric
curve (multipliers 1, .1, .01, .001). Every candidate retains the complete
canonical correction and reset; no reduced filter or force is substituted.

The repository selection authority chooses the first adjacent pair that passes
on both partitions: all nominal rows healthy; no unflagged stress failures;
no new stress failure under stronger protection; maximum component change
abs(stronger-weaker)/(1+abs(weaker)) <= .001. Components are likelihood, score,
filtering means/covariances and post-reset means/covariances. This is an
engineering non-harm criterion for controls, not a likelihood materiality or
posterior accuracy threshold. Its .1% unit-normalized scale is an inherited
screen, explicitly tested here rather than presumed transferable. A failure
triggers inspection of the measured curve, not rejection of surrogate HMC.

Nominal situations are all nine pairs in {-log(2),0,log(2)}^2. Stress situations
are all nine pairs in {-3log(2),0,3log(2)}^2, covering low/high process and
measurement scales. Results are retained by situation and row. These prior-based
points are not represented as posterior coverage. Report all numerical status
and correction diagnostics. Runtime and curve ordering are descriptive; there
is no statistical ranking of stochastic algorithms in this pass.

Fresh fixture seeds 1701 and 2701 define calibration and validation; seed 3701
defines an untouched future claim dataset. These are deterministic disjoint
stream identifiers, not selected for favorable behavior. Each is generated by
the existing simulator and observations are rounded once to FP32, then preserved
as exact inputs for all later precisions. Randomized Halton designs use the
existing seed 20260923 and partitions 0/1/2. Claim data is saved but never
evaluated or selected on in this pass. Old seed 701 evidence remains historical
qualification evidence for its own data, not the new claim.

Selection artifacts bind model, data regime, particle count, precision/backend,
chunk, full control family, source hashes and calibration/validation evidence.
A scoped selection licenses only an imperfect proposal-field experiment.
Independent posterior reference, full-support endpoint, public raw-coordinate
HMC tuning, sampling health and posterior comparisons remain required.

Hard continuation vetoes: source drift, corrupted/misidentified inputs,
unflagged nonfinite output, missing diagnostics, wrong execution device, or
budget exhaustion. Rejected settings and absence of a stable pair are candidate
repair triggers. Report original validity flags; do not map numerical invalidity
to zero posterior mass. No Gaussian prior truncation at an alias bound is allowed.

The later sampler comparison will construct prior-only force, an approximate
Gaussian force, and reference-gradient HMC where feasible as simple comparators,
and evaluate central and tail situations separately. Their dominance check is
NOT_EVALUATED here; no sampler efficiency or default-readiness claim is eligible.
