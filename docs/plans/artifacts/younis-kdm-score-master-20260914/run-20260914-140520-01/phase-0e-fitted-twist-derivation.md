# Frozen recursively fitted psi-APF

Source inspected: local Guarniero--Johansen--Lee paper, equations (5)--(6), Proposition 1, Algorithms 2--4 and section 5.1 equations (15)--(16). The official publication and Warwick copy were checked during this continuation; no original-author implementation was located. This implementation has paper anchors but cannot claim author-code parity.

Choose psi_t(x)=N(x;c_t,V_t)+b_t with positive-definite V_t and b_t>0. For the physical additive Gaussian transition N(x';f_theta(x),Q_theta), its integral is tilde_psi_t(x)=N(c_t;f_theta(x),Q_theta+V_t)+b_t. Thus the normalized twisted transition is a two-component mixture. Its Gaussian component has weight p=N(c_t;f_theta(x),Q_theta+V_t)/tilde_psi_t(x), mean f_theta(x)+K_t(c_t-f_theta(x)), and covariance Q_theta-K_t Q_theta, where K_t=Q_theta(Q_theta+V_t)^{-1}. The other component has weight 1-p and is the physical transition. The code tests this density against f psi/tilde_psi directly.

For the repository timing convention, x0 is unobserved and sampled from its physical initial law. Weight/resample x0 by f_theta(x0,psi_1), then at t sample f_theta^psi and weight by g_theta(y_t|x_t) f_theta(x_t,psi_{t+1})/psi_t(x_t). The final future factor equals one. In the joint density the psi and tilde_psi factors telescope, including the initial normalizer. This is a correctly normalized marginal-likelihood estimator under the usual integrability assumptions and multinomial resampling law. Its locally fixed-label finite-program derivative is not an unbiased model score.

Offline fitting uses Algorithm 3's recursion log target_i=log g_theta(y_t|xi_t)+log f_theta(xi_t,psi_{t+1}), proceeding backwards through a stored forward cloud. Fit a full quadratic in xi by scaled QR least squares. A positive-definite fitted precision defines the Gaussian; the constant coefficient is irrelevant because multiplying each psi_t by a positive scalar cancels in the corrected filter. A floor b_t equal to an explicit ratio times the Gaussian peak guarantees a positive prior-mixture component. Reject rank-deficient or nonpositive-precision fits; do not clip eigenvalues or silently add a ridge.

This log fit and fixed iteration count are local adaptations of the general recursive approximation and iteration idea. They do not reproduce the paper's density-scale equation (15) minimization or its adaptive particle-count/stopping rule. For d greater than the observed informative rank, the terminal full-state log fit can be rank deficient; such rows remain unsupported until an explicit subspace or regularized family is derived and evaluated. The first complete fitted fixture uses d=o=1. A useful extension is not claimed as a paper result.

During score evaluation, centers, covariances and floors are frozen, and new particle streams are independent of fitting. Differentiate Q, the physical transition/observation functions, initial law, the normalizer, conditional Gaussian mean/covariance and weight corrections. Do not differentiate fitted constants or categorical choices; report the derivative target accordingly. The fit can depend on the observed dataset as offline proposal construction, but final particle randomization is independent. Hyperparameters and fit iteration budget must be selected without final oracle losses, and any scientific held-out row must consume a scope-bound fitted artifact.

The first tests verify the proposal identity, known quadratic recovery, invalid-fit rejection, terminal observation conditioning and all-six-parameter finite-value derivatives. The no-ridge choice preserves the explicit fitted family and rejects invalid fits; this is not authority to call an ill-conditioned result valid. The positive floor is an untuned mechanics hypothesis whose downstream effect requires later calibration. This slice establishes mechanics only and cannot promote score quality or a default.
