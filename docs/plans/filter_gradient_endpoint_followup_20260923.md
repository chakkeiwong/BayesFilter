The two existing joint-center watchdog exceptions are confined to the explicitly
selected non-JIT wall-time mode; both configs reject `max_wall_seconds` with JIT.
Their previous rationale said optimizer-wide XLA was outside F18. That stale
scope assertion is removed. It cannot exempt the numerical stages above, and
the exception count/AST permissions have not increased.

The pending initializer agreement and GPU availability do not prevent source
review or preparation of these segments. Before each segment's numerical unit,
freeze its exact original authority and consumer cases and allocate a bounded
worker reservation from the unchanged campaign caps. A Python API that cannot
be represented by a tensor contract requires a documented boundary; replacing
its scientific behavior or dropping callbacks is not an execution repair.

its own evidence. Austria training helpers pack heterogeneous cores and tangent

function name. No Zhao-Cui source-faithfulness claim or algorithm change follows
from this review.
