## What is not a demonstrated omission

The audit did not find a paper that makes a generic ambient Gaussian KDM score
valid for a deterministic or singular DSGE transition. Nor did it find a
published theorem that converts Younis's fixed-mixture IWSG gradient into the
exact marginal score merely by adding UKF/GenUT/LEDH covariance updates. These
remain derivation and experiment questions, not missing citations to be
silently imported.
