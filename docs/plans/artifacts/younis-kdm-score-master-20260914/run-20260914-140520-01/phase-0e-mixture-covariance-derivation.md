# Persistent mixture covariance provider

The candidate maintains a separate Gaussian mixture for proposal moments. It is a local assumed-density filter with a KDM representation, not a reproduction of Younis' learned filter. It does not claim an unbiased model-score gradient from IWSG.

At time t-1 the mixture has normalized positive weights a_i, means m_i and covariances C_i. Apply the shared quadrature prediction to each component, obtaining (m_i^-,C_i^-). Approximate its joint state/observation distribution by the same quadrature rule. Let the resulting observation mean and covariance be z_i,S_i and the cross covariance be B_i. Conditioning gives K_i=B_i S_i^{-1}, m_i^+=m_i^-+K_i(y_t-z_i), and C_i^+=C_i^--K_i S_i K_i^T. Component evidence is e_i=N(y_t;z_i,S_i); update a_i^+=a_i e_i/sum_j a_j e_j. These are exact component updates for affine Gaussian dynamics and observations. In a nonlinear model they define a Gaussian assumed-density approximation.

For either predicted or posterior components, the aggregate covariance is P=sum_i a_i[C_i+(m_i-m)(m_i-m)^T], with m=sum_i a_i m_i. Both within-component covariance and between-component scatter are included. Differentiation uses da_i=a_i[dlogit_i-sum_j a_j dlogit_j], dm=sum_i(da_i m_i+a_i dm_i), and the full product rule for P. The component log-evidence derivative is -dz_residual^T S_i^{-1}z_residual - tr(S_i^{-1}dS_i)/2 + z_residual^T S_i^{-1}dS_i S_i^{-1}z_residual/2. The shared update returns that derivative using the same innovation and factorization as conditioning.

Initialize 2d equally weighted components at m0+sqrt(d(1-b)) L0 e_j and its negative for j=1,...,d, each with covariance b P0, where 0<b<=1. This has aggregate mean m0 and covariance P0. For b<1 it is a mixture approximation to the initial Gaussian law; b=1 gives identical Gaussian components and recovers the Gaussian filter. The model's actual LEDH initial particles still come from its physical initial law. Thus this approximation affects proposal moments, not the physical initial-law numerator.

Precompute the component recursion for the observations and use its predicted and posterior aggregate moments in the existing LEDH executor. The auxiliary filter retains its own component identities; LEDH's Contract E reset acts on the particle cloud separately. No reset of LEDH particles is asserted to update this independent mixture. The actual physical transition/observation numerator and flow proposal correction remain in the shared executor. The returned derivative includes dependence through the auxiliary filter, initial mixture, component evidence weights, flow and reset. The candidate is not stamped as canonical UKF LEDH.

Evidence contract: verify b=1 against Kalman moments, exact initial moments for b<1, observation-dependent component weights, persistence of conditioned components, analytical derivatives against the same finite scalar, and executable consumer wiring. Nonpositive component covariances fail closed with a relative precision/dimension margin; zero numerical ridge preserves the stated quadrature moments. The first b=.5 run is a mechanics hypothesis, not a selected default. Differences in covariance error or a tiny score error do not establish score improvement.
