## Required content of the next numerical protocol

Phase 7 must produce a single costed protocol for the next research decision:

1. Keep the SV filtering question explicit. Start within d=1,4 and the current
   physical target; identify every changed horizon, particle/reference count,
   dtype, backend, rank, degree, row design, L1 rule or fitting schedule.
   Tune each materially different scope rather than silently transfer d4 to d1.
2. Separate optimization at a frozen target/objective/capacity from increased
   representational capacity. Define how initialization and unequal coefficient
   counts are controlled or limit attribution. Diagnose actual recursive
   incoming TT laws as well as the Gaussian first-transition test. Derive or
   justify numerical convergence tolerances; KKT alone cannot certify a global
   nonconvex optimum, population fit, or filtering benefit.
3. Specify dedicated coverage/non-harm evaluation for the defensive Gaussian
   mass. Positive support justifies a nonzero component, not the value 0.05.
   Declare the changed proposal law, exact density/correction, healthy and
   stressed checks, selection rule and tuning scope before changing a value.
   Heuristic wins or primary-error improvement are not safety-calibration goals.
4. Freeze fresh observation-sequence partitions, training/validation/audit row
   seeds and selection rules before execution. Existing diagnostic/downstream
   observations remain exposed evidence. Reuse is permissible only as labeled
   diagnostic evidence, never as a new holdout. Keep final sequences untouched
   until controls are frozen. Fix what happens after a failed holdout.
5. Preserve the comparator ladder: transition bootstrap, stationary prior,
   SGQF Gaussian, predictive scalar TT, guided scalar TT and the pair candidate.
   Evaluate near-zero, ordinary and large observations separately. Define
   regime coverage, reference accuracy, paired unit of replication, uncertainty
   method, precision target and multiplicity treatment before observing results.
   Few/absent regime observations or inadequate precision cannot become success.
6. Classify promotion criteria, promotion vetoes, continuation vetoes, repair
   triggers and explanatory diagnostics. Preserve target/proposal validity and
   reference checks. A fitting failure rejects the tested realization; a
   downstream heuristic loss blocks promotion without rejecting the research
   direction. Neither may silently relax a gate or trigger unplanned retuning.
7. Give exact executable commands, call-chain checks, versioned artifacts,
   environment/GPU-growth provenance, data versions, per-stage cost estimates,
   total wall/attempt ceilings and stop conditions. Charge startup, compilation,
   fits, references, diagnostics and retries. Use measured end-to-end costs:
   the 48.464704-second cached-fit retry does not price fresh fitting.
8. Review the completed protocol and resolve material scientific findings.
   Obtain owner authorization for any additional compute or scope; explicitly
   decide whether the old time balance is carried forward. Update the master
   and checkpoint before the first numerical launch. No repeated human approval
   is needed for repairs within the admitted unchanged contract and total budget.

Pair total gradients, fitting derivatives, HMC, larger dimensions, coupled
