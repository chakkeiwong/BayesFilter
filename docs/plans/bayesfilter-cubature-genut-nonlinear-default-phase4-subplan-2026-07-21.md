## Question

Can antithetic initial/process innovations reduce score variance for the
candidate route while each constituent cloud remains the same finite value and
score program?

## Scope

- Target: exact transformed-SV adapter from Phase 3;
- horizon: `T=4` diagnostic prefix;
- particle counts: `N=12` and `N=24`;
- design: positive Cubature residual;
- seeds: 16 independent seed labels;
- arithmetic: float32, CPU-hidden diagnostic execution, no XLA;
- comparison: independent cloud scores versus the arithmetic mean of scores
  from a cloud and its sign-reflected initial/process innovations.

## Evidence Contract

The primary diagnostic is the across-seed sample standard deviation of each
score coordinate. Antithetic pairing is a nomination signal only: it may be
used in a later claim only if the paired estimator and its probability/finite
target contract are explicitly declared. No superiority or exact-filtering
claim follows from this diagnostic.

Pass if antithetic score standard deviation is lower on the declared fixture
without nonfinite values, replay mismatch, or reset/marginal failure. A failure
is evidence against this variance remedy for this scope, not against Cubature
or the generic score core.
