## 1. Executive verdict

The standalone rewrite is now strong enough to be treated as the **replacement candidate for canonical development promotion**.

This is not the same as claiming that the monograph is publication-perfect or that every theorem-level source gap has been closed. It means something narrower and honest:

- the rewrite is materially better than the current canonical `docs/` source,
- it builds from tracked inputs,
- its active citations and labels resolve,
- its major mathematical blockers have either been repaired or converted into explicit conditional/nonclaim language,
- and its remaining gaps are now documented as bounded nonclaims rather than hidden contradictions.

The old canonical `docs/` tree is no longer the better development baseline. Promotion should therefore proceed by **curated diff**, not by raw subtree replacement.
