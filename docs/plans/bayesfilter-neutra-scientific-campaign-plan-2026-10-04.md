# Scientific NeuTra training campaign: simple targets and randomized mixtures

Status: revision 2 repair and execution, October 4, 2026. The revision below
supersedes the original procedure where they differ. Campaign r1 is preserved
as an invalid-screen diagnostic; its final failure labels are not scientific
rejections. See the companion r1 audit in the campaign results note.
This campaign extends the completed six-method mechanics/pricing phase. It
tests training of the canonical NeuTra IAF on fixed warped/unwarped mixtures
and on unseen two- and three-center mixtures whose centers and variances are
drawn prospectively from declared intervals.

The active checkout has substantial unrelated dirty work. Preserve those
changes. The existing mechanics campaign remains historical evidence and is
not overwritten or silently reused as scientific training evidence.

## Research question and evidence contract

The main question is whether a frozen, target-agnostic training procedure can
produce a useful canonical IAF map from each method's posterior-teacher bank
on simple multimodal targets and on newly drawn two- and three-center targets.
The methods are FAB, Gabrié, annealed AIS, annealed SMC, AFT and CRAFT. The
scientific object is the trained map and its downstream target behavior; native
sampler metrics alone cannot promote a map.

The fixed targets are the existing two-component unwarped mixture and its
quadratic-shear warped version. The generalization family uses independent
random targets generated by `random_mixture_specification` with pairwise
center distances Uniform[6,10], component variances Uniform[0.5,2], random
rotation/translation, and at least 0.1 component weights. Calibration uses
separate random seeds 9101 (two components) and 9201 (three components).
Final assessment uses seeds 1101, 1102 (two components) and 2101, 2102 (three
components). The exact specifications are saved before training; learners
receive only dimension, density, score and signature through `DensityTarget`.

Each final target/method is attempted with three independent training seeds
(41, 42, 43). This is an exploratory replication screen, not a reliability
claim. The same six final target specifications are shared across methods;
method streams, initialization and teacher randomness remain independent.

The primary map screen requires, for a trial whose teacher passes its separate
screen: finite batched training and frozen-map artifacts; a complete 1,000
point latent probe; finite held-out target evaluation; and no worse held-out
forward cross-entropy than the declared identity-map baseline within the
predeclared tolerance. A coverage screen compares component responsibility
masses, weighted physical moments and tail-feature means against an independent
exact evaluator population with uncertainty summaries. The numeric limits are
screening hypotheses, recorded in every manifest, not theorems: teacher ESS
fraction >=0.20, maximum responsibility-mass discrepancy <=0.15, and map
feature z-score <=5 when the reference standard error is nonzero. A failed
screen blocks promotion of that trial and identifies the teacher, fitting or
geometry stage; it does not reject the algorithm family.

The exact-teacher control trains the same canonical IAF from independent iid
target draws. It is diagnostic evidence that separates teacher failure from
map-fitting failure and is not a replacement for the method's native teacher.
The 1,000-point score/geometry probe is explanatory only. No finite probe,
training loss, acceptance, ESS or short-chain moment establishes posterior
correctness, exhaustive mode discovery, method superiority, HMC convergence,
state-space transfer or q20 readiness.

## Frozen procedure and numerical choices

The candidate profile is intentionally bounded and explicit. Its numbers are
engineering hypotheses priced by the previous controls and calibrated before
final targets:

| Quantity | Calibration candidates | Final frozen choice rule |
|---|---|---|
| Posterior particles | 256 and 512 | Choose the smallest candidate whose independent teacher passes all hard screens; retain the selected value in the procedure manifest |
| Temperature stages | 8 and 16 | Choose by teacher ESS/feature screens; AIS and SMC share the ladder in the matched control, with resampling differing only for SMC |
| Mutation steps | 4 and 8 | Choose the smallest passing movement/finite-state profile |
| Gabrié walkers/steps | (16,4) and (32,8) | Choose by independent frozen-bank screens; preserve global-MH then local-MALA ordering |
| AFT inner updates | 2 and 4 | Choose by held-out free-energy progress without using final targets |
| CRAFT passes | 2 and 4 | Choose by fresh-pass held-out progress; stage updates remain after the complete pass |
| IAF updates | 512 and 1,024 | Choose by exact-teacher held-out cross-entropy and coverage on calibration targets; no stopping at a single noisy loss |
| IAF batch | 64 | Batch-native TensorFlow/XLA update; no sample-wise target loop |
| Learning rate | 3e-4 and 1e-3 | Select by calibration held-out criterion, with gradient/update finiteness and clipping telemetry |
| Conditional scale cap | 2.0 | Canonical source profile; saturation is explanatory and cannot be tuned on final targets |
| Proposal scale | 4.0 and 8.0 | Choose by teacher weight/cESS and physical feature screens; the broad component is retained |
| Fit seeds | 41, 42, 43 | Independent replications, not pooled iid observations |

The calibration grid is limited to two candidates per method and two
development targets. Candidate selection is frozen before any final random
target specification is assessed. If no candidate passes, that method has a
calibration failure and its final cells remain visible as prerequisite
failures. A method's success never cancels another method's row.

## Phase structure and stop conditions

1. **Preflight and source freeze.** Capture the complete dependency closure,
   verify the exact launcher rules, run CPU mechanics tests and one trusted
   GPU/XLA memory-growth probe. Stop if source hashes, memory policy, target
   signatures or executable identity are inconsistent.
2. **Calibration.** Run the finite candidate grid on the two development
   targets, including the exact-teacher control. Freeze selected profiles and
   record measured cost. Stop calibration for a method after every candidate
   fails its declared hard screen or after its bounded retry allowance.
3. **Final teacher and map trials.** Generate a fresh native teacher bank,
   qualify it independently, train the canonical IAF for the selected update
   rung, save optimizer/map/RNG state, and run held-out target and geometry
   diagnostics. Continue independent cells after a candidate failure.
4. **Refresh and terminal audit.** After each cell update `state.json`,
   `matrix.json`, `next-phase.json` and `pricing.json`. Verify all target
   specifications, source hashes, manifests, required artifacts, budget
   charges and nonpromotion fields. A terminal audit can fail without
   relaunching scientific workers.

Hard continuation vetoes are corrupted source/target artifacts, missing
required evaluator or derivative paths, failed GPU memory policy, exhausted
campaign allocation, or invalid shared mathematics. A candidate failure,
poor teacher, poor map or unresolved FAB tail applicability is a recorded
scientific outcome and repair trigger, not a veto on the entire study.

## Skeptical review before execution

The plan was red-teamed before implementation. The main ways it could produce
a misleading success are: a teacher bank misses a mode while its ESS remains
high; the exact-teacher control is mistaken for native-method evidence; a
finite probe is treated as uniform whitening; three fit seeds are treated as
independent target replications; or the random generator is consulted by the
learner through centers, weights or labels. The procedure prevents these
interpretations by keeping evaluator truth outside `DensityTarget`, saving
independent exact-reference summaries, separating teacher and map screens,
sharing final targets across methods, preserving per-seed rows, and labeling
all ranking and reliability claims as unsupported at this scale.

The inherited numbers are not silently promoted: proposal scale, particles,
ladder, mutation effort, learning rate, clipping ceiling and update rungs are
calibration candidates or bounded hypotheses. The study can show that the
current budget is insufficient; it must not convert an incomplete matrix into
a positive scientific claim. A failed method row remains a failure of the
current procedure or applicability unless a mathematical/shared-harness veto
is demonstrated.

The implementation review must check that the master reaches the actual
canonical trainer, that calibration selection precedes final target assessment,
that source/target hashes are in every manifest, that the map checkpoint is
reloaded before held-out evaluation, that no NumPy computation enters the
training path, and that the exact wrapper commands match the installed rules.

## Expected terminal report

The report must include every calibration and final cell, exact target
specifications and seeds, selected profiles, teacher screens, held-out map
metrics, 1,000-point diagnostics, hard vetoes, descriptive differences,
resource costs, uncertainty status, and explicit nonclaims. It must state
whether each failure invalidated the harness, the target, the teacher, the
fitting procedure or only the current candidate. It must not rank methods from
three seeds or promote a default without an independent downstream check.

## Revision 2: repair identified mathematical and execution defects

The question and six method families are unchanged. The first execution exposed
an invalid uncertainty calculation: teacher discrepancies were divided by only
the independent reference standard error. For independent iid populations with
sizes n and m, Var(mean(X)-mean(Y)) is Var(X)/n + Var(Y)/m, not Var(Y)/m.
At n=1024 and m=4096 with equal variances, the old statistic is inflated by
sqrt(5). An exact iid control was rejected. The same omission affects map draws.
For resampled particles or Markov trajectories, row-wise iid standard errors
would also be wrong. Revision 2 therefore uses four independent complete native
populations, conditional on the fixed initialization configuration. If h_r is
the weighted feature mean from run r, the variance estimate for their average
is sum((h_r-mean(h))^2)/(R*(R-1)); add the independent reference variance/m.
This includes within-population dependence without assuming independent rows.
R=4 is a convenience-chosen exploratory replication budget. Its variance
estimate is noisy; a five-standard-error screen is not a calibrated hypothesis
test, confidence interval, or proof of equilibrium/unbiased sampling. Persistent
bias can survive replication. The absolute responsibility discrepancy <=0.15
and minimum individual-population weight-ESS fraction >=0.20 remain separate
screens. Include local first/second moments and radial tail features from the
existing exact evaluator, rather than omitting the planned tail diagnostics.

Map draws are iid conditional on a frozen map, so combine their sample-mean
variance with that of a separate exact reference. The map screen remains
cross-entropy delta <=0.25, feature discrepancy <=5 combined standard errors,
finite artifacts and all 1,000 probe rows. Responsibility discrepancy <=0.15
also applies. The Gaussian identity is a declared weak baseline only: this
screen tests viability, not superiority over a tuned classical density. Record
cross-entropy uncertainty and normalized-target forward/reverse KL estimates;
none is a proof of whitening. Probe residual magnitudes are explanatory.

## Revision 3: budget-ladder repair after exact-teacher calibration

Revision 2 completed with a passing artifact audit but no qualified student.
All four exact-teacher calibration banks passed. Both student configurations
failed on local distribution features, with zero clipped updates. On the
two-center development target, the observed forward KL was 0.783 after the
width-16/512-update rung and 0.425 after width-32/1,024; this is descriptive and
confounds width with effort. RKL subsequently changed the component masses by
up to 0.178 (two centers) and 0.255 (three centers), exceeding the 0.15 screen.
The failure rejects these candidates, not the direction. No new final random
target was assessed in r2. The next planned repair is therefore additional
training/capacity calibration before admitting any teacher-method final trial.

Campaign r3 preserves r1 and r2 and charges their full costs against the same
ceiling. At its start 4,136.0875 GPU-process and 9,092.6995 CPU-core seconds
remain. Use width 32 / 4,096 forward updates and width 64 / 8,192 forward
updates, LR 1e-3, followed by the same 256-update RKL at 3e-4. These are
explicit geometric budget/capacity rungs, not tuned defaults. Keep the canonical
three-stage author architecture. They probe remaining optimization/capacity
failure; simultaneous width/effort changes do not identify a cause separately.
Do not alter any screen to obtain a pass. Save heldout learning diagnostics at
512, 1,024, 2,048, 4,096 and (where applicable) 8,192 updates. These reuse the
development validation partition and are explanatory. Always assess the final
predeclared checkpoint, with no best-checkpoint selection on final targets.
Preserve the full forward and RKL endpoints to expose coverage regression.

Skeptical review passes for this bounded repair: the exact teachers isolate
fitting from teacher bias, all previous smaller candidates are preserved,
fresh generalization targets are still unobserved, and the remaining allocation
can price the longer fits before admitting the full matrix. A correct negative
outcome is insufficient training/capacity under these tested rungs, not a
theorem that NeuTra cannot fit mixtures. Numerical failure, an invalid frozen
artifact or budget exhaustion still stops execution. A failed map alone does
not invalidate the harness or justify returning to the superseded screen.
