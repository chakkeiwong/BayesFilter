### Mathematical comparator and assumption audit

For the funnel, let v ~ N(0,9), z_1,z_2 iid N(0,1), and
x_i=exp(v/2) z_i. The map (v,z) -> (v,x) has determinant exp(v).
Consequently log p(v,x(v,z)) + v equals log N(v;0,9) plus
sum_i log N(z_i;0,1). This is an exact coordinate change of the existing law.
A new validation fixture uses the ordinary exact-score tuner on (v,z), then
maps posterior draws back to (v,x). It is not a new public transport codec or
an automatically selected coordinate transformation. Tests must check the
Jacobian equality, independent reference density, score, model starts and
reported model-coordinate quantities. The centered failure stays in its own
arm; a noncentered success cannot retroactively repair its denominator.

For an identity-metric Gaussian and velocity Verlet, write a complete L-step
proposal as q'=a q+b p, p'=c q+a p, with c=-(1-epsilon^2/4)b and
 a^2-bc=1. Then delta H=epsilon^2 (||q'||^2-||q||^2)/8.
Reversing the MH ratio changes the stationary position precision to
1-epsilon^2/2 (when positive and the proposal is nondegenerate), instead of 1.
This derivation predicts variance ratios 1.04712 at epsilon .3 and 1.21951 at
.6; finite K^32 observations need not yet attain those limits. At a correct
Gaussian null, independent anchor endpoints remain iid N(0,I_2), so their
summed squared norm is chi-square with 2n degrees of freedom. A two-sided exact
tail probability adds a sensitive energy test without treating adjacent chain
states as independent. Bonferroni covers the two radius tests plus this energy
test, family size three. Null/no-op experiments check the entire implementation.
This is a fixed-look test, not Gandy--Scott Algorithm 3 or sequential testing.
