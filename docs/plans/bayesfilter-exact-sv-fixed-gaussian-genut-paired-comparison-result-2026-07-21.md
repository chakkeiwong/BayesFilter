## Outcome

The reviewed paired scalar exact-SV campaign completed on GPU/XLA/TF32 for
`T=50`, `N={1002,1998}`, 4 and 8 Sinkhorn steps, and 16 common particle seeds.
All dense-reference, design-moment, recursive-score, placement, memory-growth,
finiteness, reset, and Sinkhorn gates passed.

The cross-dataset criterion was invalid because one arm was not generated by
the SV DGP.  After removing that planning error, the scientifically eligible
result is a statistically supported GenUT gamma/OPG improvement on one fresh
SV-DGP sequence.  This is nomination evidence requiring multi-DGP replication,
not a population ranking or default decision.

Both designs used the same seed-specific rank coupling, particle noise,
observations, epsilon, ridge, and Sinkhorn count.  GenUT was fixed and

recursive score.  This result does not cover adaptive moment-estimated GenUT.

## Paired Evidence

At `N=1998`, eight Sinkhorn steps:

| Data | Quantity | GenUT-minus-Cubature statistic | Paired bootstrap 95% CI | Interpretation |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Fresh DGP | Absolute gamma mean error | `-0.11955` | `[-0.14872, -0.07929]` | supported GenUT reduction |
| Fresh DGP | Absolute beta mean error | `-0.00922` | `[-0.04730, 0.05154]` | no resolved change |
| Fresh DGP | Mean per-seed OPG error | `-0.01834` | `[-0.03216, -0.00411]` | supported GenUT reduction |

The same fresh-DGP gamma reduction was supported at `N=1002` and with four
Sinkhorn steps.  On the fresh sequence, cumulative gamma error was already
smaller under GenUT by observation 10 (`0.0155` versus `0.0439`) and remained
smaller through observation 50 (`0.0331` versus `0.1526`).

## Value Interpretation

All four scientifically eligible GenUT value intervals across both particle
counts and Sinkhorn counts included zero with half-width below `0.25`.  The
fresh-DGP Cubature `N=1998` interval detected a small positive mean error around
`0.064`, approximately `0.055%` of the log-value magnitude.  GenUT was centered
on the fresh-DGP dense value.

The paired absolute-value improvement interval still included zero, so the
current evidence does not statistically rank GenUT above Cubature for value.

## Negative-Result Classification And Red Team

- Implementation failure: no evidence after the localized static-shape repair.
- Tuning failure: not evaluated; fixed shared controls deliberately removed
  method-specific selection from the causal comparison.
- Diagnostic failure: no evidence; dense refinement and same-scalar derivative
  gates passed.
- Evidence against the idea: not established from one eligible DGP sequence;
  fixed Gaussian GenUT remains viable and materially helpful on that sequence.

The strongest alternative explanation for the fresh-DGP result is favorable
dataset-specific cancellation or residual-row association, not kurtosis alone.
The result would become substantially stronger if the paired gamma/OPG
reduction reproduced across independently generated SV datasets.  It would be
weakened if those reductions changed sign or if posterior-region value errors
became material.  The current experiment says nothing about exact MH targeting.
