## Evidence contract and research intent

Comparators are the exact saved failed maps/teachers, their previous executed
settings, the moment-matched Gaussian, the normalized Laplace proposal, and
exact analytic benchmark distributions. The conditional checks are the funnel
neck versus typical scales, the mixture modes versus valley, stationary versus
changing acceptance sequences, and improving versus plateaued training.
These are constructed controls, not alternative optimization targets.

Engineering pass: the actual consumer executes the declared repair and its
focused regression and numerical check pass. Scientific pass: the unchanged
independent teacher/shape screens, frozen-sampler checks, and fresh sequential
HMC posterior checks pass for the specified target/seed. Acceptance, loss,
score residuals, ESS of importance weights, and short proposals explain or
nominate; they do not establish convergence or a method ranking. Default or
q20 readiness and exhaustive mode discovery are not conclusions of this study.

Nonfinite arithmetic, incorrect change of variables/weights, stale parent
identity, corrupt checkpoints, missing required evidence or exceeded budgets
stop dependent execution. A failed map, teacher or tuning candidate triggers
its declared repair or a recorded unresolved research outcome, not termination
of independent work. Never relax a scientific screen to make a row pass.

## Repairs, verification and execution order

1. **HMC consumer and effective search.** Generate starts through the frozen
   canonical map from seeded standard-normal latent draws; archive both spaces,
   target values, scores and round-trip error. Do not convert density modes to
   chain starts by isotropic physical jitter. Make downward repair count an
   explicit campaign setting and serialize effective settings before tuning.
   Use 8 repairs initially and 12 for the expanded retry: these are bounded
   search hypotheses, extending the old 3-halving range to factors 256 and 4096,
   not a guarantee of useful step sizes. Keep epsilon .5 and L=3/9/18 as the
   inherited starting hypotheses and let the existing public tuner measure
   their suitability. Verify the outer consumer's actual start bank and tuner
   arguments, plus a stiff numerical fixture requiring a lower epsilon.
   Identity latent mass and the shared sequential HMC policy remain required.

2. **Temporal evidence.** Retain historical raw-block behavior for historical
   policies. Add an explicit campaign opt-in that tests the six pairwise block
   contrasts across four independent chains, using paired Student intervals
   with a familywise 0.01 working level (Bonferroni over six contrasts, df=3).
   A contrast must both exceed the practical-band width and exclude zero to
   trigger temporal inconclusiveness. The width is the existing material
   acceptance tolerance; 0.01 is a conservative diagnostic hypothesis, not a
   calibrated universal error guarantee. Across-chain pairing retains within-
   chain dependence in the block differences. It needs empirical calibration
   because four-chain Student inference is approximate.
   Before campaign use, test 2048 seeded stationary replications at 64 and
   256 decisions/chain, including persistent Markov dependence, and strong
   common temporal shifts as positive controls. The calibration screen is
   false temporal flags <=5% and detection >=80% for the declared strong-shift
   controls; these are explicit sensibility screens, not method-accuracy or
   nominal-coverage claims. Preserve all numerical/movement vetoes. If the
   screen fails, revise and rerun calibration before dependent HMC; never
   reinterpret historical candidates as admitted. The global legacy default
   is not silently replaced.

3. **Training continuation, plateau repair and observability.** Distinguish
   numerical failure, shape failure with meaningful progress, plateau, and
   exhausted allocation. Continue the actual map, Adam variables, iteration
   count and Gabrié walkers from a saved checkpoint. Measure paired validation
   log-density changes between distinct maps, with their standard error;
   inherited 3-SE evidence nominates progress but is not a method ranking.
   Preserve gradient-norm totals and clipped-update counts at every rung.
   Extend improving saved fits at 16384, 32768 and 65536 lifetime updates,
   bounded by price and campaign budget. The doubling ladder is a work limit,
   not a universal training prescription. Start with one improving mixture
   checkpoint per available seed, in fixed width/LR order.
   Test Gaussian plateaus separately: matched continuation control versus a
   256-update standard-RKL escape followed by forward fitting of the same
   teacher. This is a declared objective-schedule experiment inside the author
   IAF, not claimed author-controller equivalence. Preserve the parent and
   allow no failed temporary map into qualification. Retain earlier useful
   maps if refinement damages coverage. Test restoration with real Adam
   updates and test the actual continuation decision for improving, flat,
   invalid and budget-limited cases.

4. **SMC mutation and proposal overlap.** Wire actual mutation work 4 ->16 ->64
   into separate repairs at fixed particle count before increasing particles.
   Record each executed beta, step, mutation count, ancestry, importance
   overlap and dimensionless displacement. Calibrating a tiny accepted step
   never closes the teacher-quality question. Preserve the failed physical
   Laplace baseline. If its proposal/geometry remains inadequate, use an
   independently trained, already assessed canonical Gabrié map as a frozen
   change of coordinates for SMC. This is an explicit rescue arm, not evidence
   that the original mode-only proposal worked and not oracle training.
   For x=T(z), run the same SMC on log p(T(z))+log|J_T(z)|, with a normalized
   defensive Gaussian/Student latent proposal. This corrects both weights and
   MALA forces in the transformed space. Returning T(z) with unchanged weights
   targets the original physical distribution by change of variables.
   Freeze pilot-calibrated step sizes before independent teacher replications;
   test several beta regions without tuning on the final reference. Verify
   the Jacobian and importance correction against an analytic transformed
   Gaussian and normalization identities, then apply the original teacher
   screen to three independent populations. If this rescue fails, preserve its
   failure and distinguish overlap, mobility and precision before further
   bounded work. No formula specific to the funnel enters the runtime sampler.

5. **Real consumer canaries then remaining matrix.** Run repaired qualification
   on the preserved funnel map and the recorded block-rule case first, using
   fresh final reference streams. Run saved-mixture continuation and a bounded
   teacher repair, checking their actual parameter/state deltas before further
   seeds. Then execute the same relevant repairs for remaining failed cases;
   successful historical cases need focused regression, not wholesale reruns.
   Use unique attempt names under this plan and source/input-aware reuse.
   Every phase refreshes next action, classified failure, cost and evidence.

6. **Terminal review.** For each audited gap, record the actual consumer,
   regression, numerical artifact and remaining scientific uncertainty as
   open, implemented/unverified, or verified for its stated scope. Recheck
   whether the old failure could still pass the new tests. Record candidate
   outcomes separately from implementation closure. Include the required
   decision and inference-status tables and update the concise checkpoint.

verified before initialization, TensorFlow/TFP batched XLA kernels and the
canonical three-stage IAF. Existing saved maps use FP64; preserve that precision
for paired repairs rather than mixing a precision experiment into this one.
CPU tests and reference generation hide GPUs. No pfor and no sample-wise scalar
training fallback are allowed. Existing author sources and the shared HMC

Initial numerical reservation: at most 4 GPU process-hours and 8 CPU
core-hours for calibration and first consumer checks, taken from the remaining
allowance. Expand only within the already authorized total after those checks
show the actual repairs execute. Individual checks start with a 600-second
wall ceiling, inherited from prior phase pricing; training continuation may
use 1800 seconds and full qualification 1200 seconds. These are engineering
ceilings, not evidence of convergence. Infrastructure retries preserve costs
and inputs and receive fresh output directories. Exhausted local work must be
reported as bounded/unresolved rather than proof that the method fails.

## Detailed skeptical review before implementation

- The previous phase-mock tests could pass while the bad numerical calls
  survived. New tests must reach the consumer and inspect the executed inputs;
  mocks may bound cost, never replace the behavior being claimed.
- A correct density mode is not a typical draw. The start repair uses the
  supplied frozen map; posterior checks still expose a map that misses mass.
- More budget is not a wider domain. Tests must inspect the downstream tuner
  configuration and produce an actually smaller candidate.
- Raw block crossings are not a statistical instability test. The optional
  contrast policy must pass predeclared null/alternative calibration and
  retain independent verification; no global numerical default changes.
- A saved checkpoint is not continuation. Check Adam state and cumulative
  iterations, preserve teacher weights, and compare distinct validation maps.
- A plateau and an improving shape failure need different repairs. The RKL
  escape arm is a hypothesis; no promise that additional updates solve IAF.
- Resampling the same poor proposal does not establish bridge coverage. The
  transformed SMC rescue uses an independent frozen map, exact correction and
  independent teacher validation. Its success cannot be attributed to the
  original Laplace proposal or to mode search alone.
- Final references must remain separate from repair decisions. Failed final
  outcomes remain archived and retries obtain new references and seeds.
- Tests and numerical pass criteria are scoped separately; no success count,
  diagnostic loss or short-chain acceptance becomes posterior evidence.

Review verdict: the plan covers all six retrospective rows and preserves the
earlier safeguards for stale reuse, failure classification, warm-map selection,
fresh references, kernel parity and budgets. It is executable as a bounded
research repair, with empirical hypotheses explicitly left subject to failure.
Proceed with focused implementation, then calibration before dependent runs.

## Review after the first executed repairs

The actual consumer canaries support continuation: preserved funnel and wiggle
maps passed fresh posterior checks; the seed-11 mixture escape plus continued
forward fit passed; plain mixture seed 37 was still improving at 65536.
The physical teacher remained inaccurate despite an executed 4 -> 16 mutation
change. A frozen-map teacher passed, with no resampling in its first checked
population, which attributes that success to proposal overlap rather than to
the configured mutation count. The new IAF trained on that teacher failed HMC
warm-up readiness, so it remains unpromoted.

The 65536 rung is a pricing checkpoint, not a terminal scientific limit.
For fits with supported paired progress and residual shape error, continue
doubling lifetime updates using preserved state while the next rung fits the
existing per-job and total campaign allowance. Stop that continuation on the
unchanged shape screen, lack of supported progress, numerical invalidity, or
actual allocated-work exhaustion. Record pending work explicitly if a priced
job cannot fit; do not restart the map or label this architectural failure.

Also exercise the original Gabrié mixture parents: seed 23 has two nonlinear
fits with saved walkers; seeds 11 and 37 have Gaussian plateaus. Use fixed
width/LR order for nominations, continue the former, and use the same matched
RKL-escape/forward protocol for the latter. A newly eligible Gabrié map needs
its separate frozen-sampler check before final HMC. This is coverage of the
already audited sampler-state restoration boundary, not a new architecture or
new campaign direction. Independent earlier pass cases remain preserved.

The final implementation review must also check that numerical failure is
reported distinctly, that failed terminal fits retain the standard 1000-point
geometry diagnostic where finite, and that mixture teacher weights match the
saved continuation parent. These observability and input checks close audit
rows; they do not change a scientific screen. Focused regression precedes
execution of any resulting source repair.

The post-run skeptical review identified one confound in the plateau experiment:
the RKL intervention starts a new forward Adam state whereas pure continuation
retains it. Add a forward-Adam-reset-only control on each nominated plateau,
with the same forward updates and data. Without that control, a passing escape
arm establishes a viable combined schedule, not that RKL rather than resetting
optimizer state caused the escape. Keep all three histories and refrain from
method ranking without replicated uncertainty evidence.

## Final follow-up audit before execution

The follow-up must read the teacher directory from the parent's recorded input
hashes, not nominate a newer teacher by filename. Both particle and weight
hashes must match before any update. Pure continuation restores Adam; the
reset-only and RKL-escape controls reset Adam explicitly while preserving the
map, forward-update lifetime and Gabrié walkers. Each control records its
actual optimizer count. A missing or changed input stops dependent work.

Finite terminal failures receive the same 1000-point probe as eligible fits;
numerical failure is reported distinctly and cannot become a plateau repair.
The probe is explanatory, not an alternative shape or posterior criterion.
Nonfinite or incomplete probe rows remain a numerical continuation veto for
that candidate, including a candidate that passes the coarse shape screen.
SMC movement is also reported relative to the coordinate variance of the
pre-mutation population. A zero variance makes that coordinate's ratio
undefined, with an explicit validity mask; it is never reported as proof of
mobility. These additions do not change candidate selection.

Follow-up traversal skips a parent when a later continuation already resolves
it. Resuming preserves incident history but clears stale current errors.
Continuation cost is priced at twice the previous measured seconds per update
plus 30 seconds of setup: a conservative engineering hypothesis checked against
actual cost, not a scientific constant. CPU and GPU allowances are both checked.
Gabrié plateaus receive pure continuation, reset-only and reset-plus-RKL controls;
eligible maps must pass the existing frozen-sampler screen before final HMC.

Audit verdict: these changes close input, failure-reporting and causal-control
gaps without weakening a screen. Focused tests must reach the real fit consumer,
check changed teacher rejection before optimization, inspect restored/reset
optimizer counts, and verify the finite-failure probe and numerical-veto path.
Implementation changes to workers wait until the currently running campaign
has ended so every numerical attempt retains a coherent source snapshot.

Final case-ledger reconciliation found one additional application of the same
continuation defect: warped-mixture/Gabrié seed 37. Its eight finite fits learned
nonlinear structure but all stopped at 8192 updates with residual shape error.
Include a fixed-order saved-parent continuation for this row, preserving Adam
and walkers and applying the same pricing, paired-progress, frozen-sampler and
fresh HMC checks. Enumerate original failed cases from the saved 30-case ledger
and require each to have an executed repair or explicit unresolved status.
Do not equate testing one example of a defect with covering every affected row.

The terminal numerical-probe veto also applies after RKL refinement. Both the
supervisor and qualification consumer reject an upstream failed selection;
