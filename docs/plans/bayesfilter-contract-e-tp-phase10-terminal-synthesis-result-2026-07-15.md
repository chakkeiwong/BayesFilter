## Zhao--Cui Comparator Verdict

No primary row has a source-route Zhao--Cui parameter-learning comparator. The
repaired scalar adjacent-state implementation is
`fixed_parameter_adjacent_state_squared_tt_extension` and is classified
`extension_or_invention`. It is derivative-correct for its own finite scalar
but inaccurate at the tested capacities. The existing SIR P90/P91 route
provides a value bridge and local complete-data component score, not the
marginal observed-data total score. The generic retained-grid route remains
historical/diagnostic and was not substituted.

Therefore the requested Zhao--Cui gradient comparison was performed wherever
mathematically eligible only as an explicitly labeled extension diagnostic;
source-route Zhao--Cui comparison cells remain unavailable rather than being
filled with a different algorithm.

## Structural/NAWM Boundary

Phase 4 proves that the teacher-projection construction can preserve selected
features and tangents on one finite singular structural fixture without adding
off-support noise. It does not establish an executable NAWM likelihood. The
master plan requires client-owned stochastic/deterministic partitions,
innovation map, deterministic completion, observations, parameter chart, and a
same-target scalar. Those inputs are absent from the frozen six-row registry.
Inventing them would change the scientific target, so the correct Phase 10
classification is an explicit adapter/comparator blocker.

## Post-Run Red Team

The strongest alternative explanation for favorable center results is
preparation overfit: charts and continuation features may work at one theta and
one deterministic dataset but fail over the parameter region or across
preparation ensembles. A held-out region failure or adjacent-resolution
instability would overturn promotion; no promotion is made. The weakest
evidence is nonlinear full-horizon feasibility, which was not executed.

The result that would most change the conclusion is a target-specific,
replicated, full-horizon, same-target study with stable adjacent resolutions,
valid parameter-region charts, a checked total score, and a trusted GPU/XLA
implementation. Until then, Contract E--TP is experimental and the complete
leaderboard/HMC claims remain blocked.
