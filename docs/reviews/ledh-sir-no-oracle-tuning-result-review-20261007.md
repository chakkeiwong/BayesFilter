# Terminal review of the SIR tuning campaign

Reviewed on 2026-10-07 against
`docs/plans/ledh-sir-no-oracle-tuning-20261006.md`, its score-localization
amendment, the retained numerical records, the runner, focused tests, and the
rendered monograph. This is a skeptical author self-review, not independent
peer review.

The bounded campaign is complete. No searched alternative satisfies every
predeclared calibration screen, so the existing guarded marginal controls
remain selected at all four horizons. The heuristic comparisons veto promotion.
The evidence does not establish improved SIR accuracy or a statistical ranking.
This rejects the tested alternatives for this selection rule, not the
marginal-mixture research direction.

The strongest alternative explanation for apparently favorable local reset
errors is that accumulated proposal or earlier filtering error dominates the
final score. The measured early relative ESS near 1/1008 and large early reset
score displacements identify a useful next question, but do not isolate a
cause. A fresh-data comparison with an independently checked full filtering
reference, together with more paired designs, could overturn the present
inability to rank candidates. The weakest evidence is the two-design heuristic
comparison and the absence of a full SIR oracle.

No further sweep is needed to finish this plan. A future repair should target
the early concentration regime with new calibration/holdout partitions and an
explicit reference strategy. The unused worker budget does not itself justify
changing the completed experiment's scope.

Terminal decision: **complete and commit the implementation, diagnostic
evidence and documentation; retain existing controls; do not promote an
accuracy improvement, new default, or HMC readiness.**
