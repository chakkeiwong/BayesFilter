# BayesFilter Academic Governance Reset

Date: 2026-07-13

Status: `ACTIVE_OWNER_DIRECTIVE`

## Decision

BayesFilter now uses the three-tier academic workflow in `AGENTS.md`. Governance
is proportional to credible scientific, computational, external, or
irreversible risk. Routine work no longer requires phase files, hash freezes,
authorized snapshots, custom guards, or repeated external reviews. Material
research work uses one concise live plan and one result/reset note. High-impact
work retains explicit approval and proportional review.

This decision supersedes earlier repository documents that made dedicated
subplans and review convergence mandatory for every step. It does not weaken:

- skeptical pre-run audits and evidence contracts for research-grade runs;
- numerical correctness and parity checks;
- statistical uncertainty and scientific-claim discipline;
- reproducible commands, environment, seeds, device/JIT provenance, and result
  artifacts for serious runs;
- GPU/CUDA trust and resource limits;
- protection of unrelated dirty-worktree changes; or
- approval requirements for destructive, external, release, model-file,
  dependency, funding, or default-policy actions.
