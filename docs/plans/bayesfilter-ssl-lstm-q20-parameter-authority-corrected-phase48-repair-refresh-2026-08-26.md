## Interpretation

The v3.0 independent-proposal ratio, candidate measure, status handling, and
pairing contract passed. The arm moved particles, but its three-bank
descriptive spread was not lower overall than identity. Because only two
independent proposals were attempted at each nonterminal stage and acceptance
remained material, this result weakens the specific two-step configuration but
does not distinguish insufficient depth from a biased or poorly overlapping
proposal law.

The MathDevMCP false counterexample came from asking about unconstrained bridge
symbols. After substituting the bridge definitions, the algebra was certified
by SymPy. This is a tool-scope limitation, not an implementation failure.

## Repair decision

Run a new paired diagnostic with eight independent-proposal MH steps per
nonterminal beta stage. Preserve the v3.0 two-step report as the exact
comparator, regenerate the same initial clouds, replay the identity endpoint,
and use the unchanged defensive mixture, target, schedule, and theta measure.
The depth arm remains role-limited and cannot establish convergence,
whitening, posterior correctness, HMC readiness, or default readiness.

## Required gates and stop rules

1. A depth fixture checks repeated beta-zero acceptance, finite ratios, and
   nonzero beta-one movement.
2. The q=20 runner must reproduce the Phase 47/48 initial and identity hashes,
   preserve the exact resampling seeds, and use the same `q(theta)` in both
   candidate generation and acceptance evaluation.
3. Invalid target/status candidates must be rejected and never replace a
   current row; all retained rows must remain finite `[256,4]` tensors.
4. The CPU report must validate the passing fixture and boundary and compare
   depth-8 spreads with the preserved depth-2 report.
5. Acceptance, ESS, roots, mode mass, and spread remain explanatory. No
   threshold or ranking is promoted.

An unavailable target/proposal, exact fixture contradiction, three unrepaired
infrastructure failures, or exhausted campaign budget is a continuation veto.
Poor whitening or a failed depth arm is a repair trigger, not a direction veto.
