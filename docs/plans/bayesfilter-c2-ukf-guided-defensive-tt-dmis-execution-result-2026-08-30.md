those were repaired and the LaTeX source was rebuilt. The executable audit then
found two additional defects:

1. TensorFlow's `stateless_gamma` parameter is a rate. The chi-square sampler

   chi-square identity.

   It now verifies and carries the resolved masses, with a regression test.

The shared APF evaluator now resolves and validates initial and transition base
mass rows, includes them in the finite scalar and recursive score, and exposes

## Red-Team Conclusion

The strongest alternative explanation is that the ESS gain comes entirely from
the transformed defensive component, making the retained TT unnecessary. The
defensive-only and Gaussian-hint arms are included precisely to expose that
possibility, and their salient-time advantage triggers the heuristic veto.
The conclusion would be overturned by a same-scalar or proposal-law failure,
an invalid snapshot identity, or a replicated reference incompatibility. The
weakest evidence is the single retained fit and the fact that the alpha/nu
pilot's point minimum was unstable; no tuned pair was replayed into final
banks.

## Alpha/Nu Pilot Follow-Up

The Phase 5 pilot was executed after the fixed-half serious run with the
separate driver
`docs/benchmarks/run_c2_ukf_guided_defensive_tt_dmis_pilot_20260830.py`.
Attempt 01 is preserved as an infrastructure-only superseded attempt because
memory growth was configured after logical-device initialization. The repaired
attempt 02 completed in `1580.03` seconds on the trusted GPU/XLA lane and is
preserved at:

`docs/benchmarks/artifacts/c2_ukf_guided_defensive_tt_dmis_20260829/pilot-attempt02/`

It used one fresh retained fit; all 19 transitions; `nu` values `4`, `8`,
`16`, and the Gaussian limit; the 33-point alpha grid; 4,096 objective draws
and an independent 4,096-draw validation bank per `(nu,time)`; and 2,000
bootstrap resamples. The objective used the exact joint `(W_j/a_j)^2` factor,
all values were finite, and all analytical second-derivative estimates were
nonnegative. The aggregate validation point minimum was `(nu=16, alpha=0.6)`
with variance `1.4544334272e-05`, compared with fixed-half variance
`1.9400602566e-05`. Its bootstrap relative standard error was `0.075244` and
the 95th-percentile variance ratio was `0.913913`, but only `0.2865` of
bootstrap minimizers retained the same tail and an alpha within one grid step,
below the predeclared `0.80` gate. The pilot therefore returned
`fallback_fixed_half`; no selected allocation or tuned final comparison was
issued.
