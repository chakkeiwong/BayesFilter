## Question and evidence

Can either permitted NeuTra procedure estimate the fixed q20/T30, four-parameter,
float64 UKF-approximate posterior with the declared numerical health, convergence,
precision, start-group consistency and independent-integration checks?

Plain NeuTra is attempted first as an operational ordering, not a claim of
superiority. It trains directly at beta one. If its assessed maps, tuning or
posterior checks fail at their declared caps, the next candidate is the tempered
NeuTra ensemble with independently rooted charts. Either method uses identity
mass in learned coordinates; neither requires classical mass adaptation.

| Role | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Pass criterion | One permitted method passes sequential posterior checks, start-group consistency and agreement with a qualified independent integration reference. |
| Candidate veto / repair trigger | Unassessed training, no independently verified kernel, failed movement/energy/target-health screen, or failed posterior/coverage/precision check. Preserve the failed candidate and proceed to the other permitted method when valid and affordable. |
| Continuation veto | Corrupted source/checkpoint/target identity, broken shared numerical implementation, missing required evidence, or exhausted total allowance. Infrastructure interruption resumes the same work; it does not turn a candidate into a scientific failure. |
| Reference repair | An unqualified reference prevents validated-estimate status; trying another sampler cannot repair reference precision. |
| Explanatory only | Training loss, acceptance, short-chain ESS, runtime and architecture differences. |
| Nonconclusions | No method ranking, superiority, exact nonlinear-likelihood claim, perfect whitening or proof of global mode coverage. |

The comparator for posterior validation is independent integration of the same
UKF posterior, not another HMC method. Existing posterior thresholds, the training
ladder and assessment, epsilon/L search and fresh kernel verification remain.
Three confirmation replications of all methods are retired; four independent
chains and their existing statistical checks remain necessary for each attempt.
Tempering is part of the ensemble algorithm. Only its beta-one stream estimates
the intended posterior.
