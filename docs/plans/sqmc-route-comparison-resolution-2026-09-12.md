# SQMC route comparison: historical interpretation withdrawn

Date: 2026-09-25. The 2026-09-12 document is retained under the repair
artifacts as historical provenance. Its claims that the four routes were
statistically equivalent, that warm-start controls were sufficient, and that a
route should be the default are withdrawn.

The cited Austria raw results were not available at the cited paths for this
audit, so their confidence intervals and pairwise conclusions cannot be
recomputed or independently checked. The 3D diagnostics had small seed counts
and inherited controls. Their cosine, Fisher-scaled, and induced-HMC labels
were mathematically invalid for the reasons documented in
sqmc-repair-mathematical-corrections-20260925.md.

The repair campaign establishes only that the affected consumers now share
matched LGSSM definitions, compute complete analytical finite-program scores,
distinguish the requested route controls, and reject invalid or mismatched
tuning evidence on checked fixtures. It does not establish route equivalence,
accuracy, statistical ranking, default readiness, HMC readiness, or an SQMC
theorem for the Contract-E/correction construction. A new claim-bearing
comparison requires exact-scope tuning, fresh disjoint data and seeds,
conditional heuristic baselines, and declared uncertainty.
