## Question, evidence and limits

Do the current public tuning interfaces, research consumers, recovery paths,
capability registry and operational documentation describe and execute the
same experimental methods? The comparator is the existing two-entry-point
contract: ordinary and fixed-transport preparation feed one candidate-set
controller, retain every verified identity and assess posterior outcomes
separately. Fixed HMC, randomized HMC, discarded SNAPER proposals and fully
refreshed MAMS are the allowed method scope. NUTS remains excluded.

The pass criterion is that focused integration, negative-control, migration,
recovery and generated-document checks pass, and the tuning chapter builds
from the current source. These are engineering criteria only. A failed test,
misleading documented capability, unresolved chapter error or omitted member
is a repair trigger. A corrupted target/source binding or an exhausted check
allocation stops the affected check. Live posterior counts, acceptance,
R-hat, ESS, MCSE and reference differences are not criteria for this audit.
No method ranking, posterior accuracy, interval coverage, optional release or
new default follows from passing. Modern profiles remain experimental.

Independent units for later statistical work remain complete fits; candidate
siblings, chains, checkpoints and this regression suite are not replications.
The running SNAPER result cannot be called complete before its terminal audit.

adapters rather than a replacement tuner. CPU tests are explicitly separated
from GPU qualification; construction checks do not establish posterior
correctness. Complete member denominators, failed references and count limits
