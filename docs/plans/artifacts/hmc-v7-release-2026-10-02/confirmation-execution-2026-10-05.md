## Question and evidence contract

Does the frozen full v7 search deliver at least one checked fixed kernel
reliably for the prepared QR LGSSM, nonlinear SSM and residual-whitened funnel?
The baseline is the complete audited source-23 development procedure, not a
single candidate or an older-policy run. Run 32 independent searches per family
on the same target/data/starts/settings, for a fixed denominator of 96. Preserve
every verified member, its export/reload and the reconstructed checkpoint.

The promotion criterion is the existing simultaneous one-sided 95% exact
binomial lower bound exceeding .80 in each family. An unsuccessful, timed-out,
resource-deferred or missing slot stays in its original denominator. Numerical
health and artifact validity veto membership or interpretation. Acceptance,
tuning R-hat, runtime and resource observations explain outcomes; they do not
establish posterior convergence, calibration of posterior intervals, comparative
superiority, automatic preparation reliability or learned-map quality. Default
promotion is outside this scoped release. The terminal audit remains required
even when the supervisor reports complete delivery.

Candidate failure triggers its already-declared repairs; it does not end the
campaign. Invalid evidence, source mismatch, memory-policy violation,
unaccounted work or exhausted budget stop the affected execution. An invalid
harness stops further sampling and preserves every unstarted slot. Localized
infrastructure repair may continue under the same science and remaining budget,
with fresh attempt directories and original slots; it cannot erase failures or
resample an unfavorable confirmation seed.
