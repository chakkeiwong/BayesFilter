## Purpose and boundary

P1 is the smallest real sequential execution after the source and authority
preflight. It tests one declared cold representative scope for the q=20
factor candidate and an independently tuned strict comparator. It deliberately
does not claim to validate the two-chart ensemble, replica exchange, chart
quality, posterior correctness, or any repository default.

The representative scope is chart 0 at beta 1.0. It is chosen because it is
the cold posterior scope and therefore exercises the target-facing sequential
controller without silently treating a beta-zero mechanics scope as posterior
evidence. The full six-scope ensemble remains a later P2/P3 question.

The September 6 factor result and reset memo are current-session provenance.
They provide the narrow backend admission only; they do not establish
sequential-sampler readiness. P1 uses the source-synchronized factor receipt,
its independent audit, the September 4 parent mechanics result, and the
current-dated reconciliation note.

The Phase 9B source-authority P0 has passed, but it is not the executable-
