\title{UKF-guided particle proposals and covariance-preserving resets\\
\large A self-contained proposal for likelihoods, analytical scores,\\
and bounded higher-moment correction}
\author{BayesFilter research note}
\date{8 October 2026}
\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
A particle filter contains several different covariances. The uncertainty
conditional on one ancestor determines an ancestor-centred proposal; the
uncertainty of the whole predictive mixture determines a common proposal; and
the covariance represented by the importance weights determines what a
resampling transform should preserve. Confusing these quantities can cause a
well-defined importance sampler to perform very poorly. We derive a filter
that gives the unscented Kalman filter (UKF) an explicit role in a common
proposal, combines that proposal with conditional local flows and an unflowed
transition component, and corrects all components with one marginal density
ratio. We give a complete covariance reset, a bounded candidate correction for
selected third and fourth moments, and the analytical derivatives of the
resulting finite computation. The identities are proved under stated
conditions. Accuracy of the combined filter, non-regression across models,
and suitability for Hamiltonian Monte Carlo remain empirical and numerical
questions; this note does not promote a new implementation.
\end{abstract}
