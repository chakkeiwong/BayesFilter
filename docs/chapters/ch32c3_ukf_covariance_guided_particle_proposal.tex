\chapter{UKF-Guided Particle Proposals and Covariance-Preserving Resets}
\label{ch:bf-ukf-covariance-proposal}
\chaptermark{UKF Proposals and Covariance Resets}

Chapter~\ref{ch:bf-ledh-pfpf-ot-custom-gradient} requires the reset to preserve
importance-weighted moments and the score to differentiate the same finite
filtering computation. We now examine how a UKF covariance can guide the
proposal while respecting those requirements. The distinction is between
uncertainty conditional on one ancestor, uncertainty across the predictive
mixture, and uncertainty represented by the importance-weighted posterior.
Each covariance has a different role in the algorithm.

The construction below is now the implemented covariance-guided proposal
route used by the five-model surrogate-HMC campaign. It combines a common UKF
guide, conditional local flows, one marginal importance correction, a
Cholesky moment reset, and a bounded optional higher-moment correction. The shared consumer is \path{make_batched_score_filter}. Its module, under
\path{bayesfilter/highdim/}, is
\begin{quote}\small
\path{covariance_proposal_score_batch_tf.py}.
\end{quote}
Every model adapter reaches that recurrence. The derivations establish the finite value and
analytical total score that the code evaluates, while the case study in
Chapter~\ref{ch:bf-surrogate-hmc-five-models} reports the separate endpoint,
Metropolis correction and posterior diagnostics. Particle matrices in this
chapter have particles as columns, \(X\in\R^{d\times N}\); this transposes
the row-particle storage convention in the preceding chapter without changing
the covariance or derivative identities.
