## Evidence contract and audit before implementation

Question: do exact downstream controls and reliable teachers permit the
canonical IAF, with continued forward coverage during RKL, to support posterior
estimation on the unwarped and warped mixtures? The normalized analytic targets
are the references. Exact versus estimated teachers, a shared forward parent,
and FKL/RKL/combined continuations isolate separate mechanisms. Pure continuation
preserves Adam; objective comparisons reset Adam identically. A combined loss
uses one gradient and one Adam update, not alternating optimizers.

Primary success is the shared fixed-transport HMC posterior assessment plus
independent physical reference agreement. The stronger broad-valley target is
relative MCSE <=20%, reported separately from legacy v4. Mode-spanning starts
and fresh confirmation streams check robustness. Known-normalizer/quantile
controls are diagnostic references, not a substitute learned architecture.
Numerical invalidity or inaccurate corrections veto the affected evidence.
Teacher/fit/kernel failures trigger their declared repairs; only shared
invalidity, unavailable hardware or exhausted allocation stops dependent work.
Loss, score residuals and acceptance explain failures and cannot promote maps.
There is no ranking, exhaustive mode-discovery, q20 or default-readiness claim.

Audit: the original proposal could confound changed teachers, objective and
Adam reset; could silently replace the exact target with author's energy
clipping; and could attribute long-run plateaus to short training. This plan
controls each. Quantile differentiation is implicit, source energy clipping is
excluded explicitly, and previous 65,536/131,072-update plateau evidence remains
relevant. The exact Gaussian HMC control is identified separately from the
numerical change-of-variables control. Both must pass. Repeated stochastic
checks are operational evidence, not anytime confidence guarantees.
