# Backward-snowball ledger

Seed sources: Poyiadjis et al. (2011), Nemeth et al. (2016), PaRIS, Murray et
al. (2013), Corenflos et al. (2021), and the two Younis--Sudderth papers.
Entries are grouped by the decision they could change.

The snowball shows that the current matrix names most concepts but under-specifies
the direct algorithms and their assumptions. “KDM”, “backward pair”, and
“coupled multilevel” are not sufficient citations or implementation contracts.
