## 1. Outcome and governing decision

The program answers one question:

> Can a fresh, auditable particle authority repair the known fixed-replay
> failure, and do ETPF, GenUT, or invertible LEDH-PFPF help when added one at a
> time without being mistaken for a posterior density or mode-discovery
> theorem?

The direction is hybrid. The wholesale change is the source of particle data:
the six-bank normalized replay is descriptive historical context only. The
candidate authority is a fresh tempered SMC/SMC-U route with explicit proposal
densities, unnormalized mass bookkeeping, invariant mutation, and a defensive
full-support component. ETPF, GenUT, LEDH-PFPF, and a full ET-PF remain
separately labeled mechanisms. No whitening, ESS, loss, or covariance metric
can promote an arm by itself.

## 4. Evidence contract

**Question.** Does a fresh authority plus modular mechanisms improve target-faithful
particle support and usable NeuTra training inputs?

**Exact comparators.** Same target, partitions, and declared seeds for C0/M0;
M1-M4 are paired to the frozen M0 protocol. Historical six-bank replay is
reported only as descriptive context and is never a pass comparator.

**Primary pass criteria.**

1. A known-density or known-mass fixture passes the exact contract claimed by
   the route.
2. Every q=20 row has finite target/status and auditable proposal/transition/
   ancestry metadata; M0 additionally has frozen-law hash and unnormalized mass.
3. Independent q=20 runs show reproducible mass/mode diagnostics under the
   predeclared uncertainty analysis. A short run can remain viable but cannot
   rank candidates.
4. Each mechanism is judged only against its role: ETPF moments plus target
   checks, GenUT selected moments plus proposal utility, LEDH density identity
   plus target checks, and ET-PF reference agreement as an approximation.
5. NeuTra is considered only after particle and transport gates; its training
   screen is GPU/XLA, batch-native, memory-policy compliant, and downstream
   exact transformed-target checks are present.

**Hard vetoes.** Crash or non-finite artifact; invalid target/status; missing
support; missing density/Jacobian term; stale or caller-stamped protocol hash;
mutation invariance failure; a scalar/row-mapped NeuTra training path; GPU
memory-growth or device-provenance failure; overwritten or incomplete run
manifest; or a role claim that exceeds the computed quantity.

**Explanatory-only diagnostics.** Whitening, covariance residual, ESS,
acceptance, bridge percentage, loss, gradient norm, runtime, and one-seed tail
quantiles. These can nominate repairs, never certify authority or superiority.

**Artifact.** Every phase writes a unique directory below
`docs/plans/artifacts/ssl-lstm-q20-particle-authority-master-2026-08-25/`
containing a command, commit, environment, seeds, device policy, wall time,
inputs, hashes, raw receipts, result note, decision table, and next-step
refresh note.

## 7. Inter-phase execution protocol

After every phase, in order:

1. Stop the phase command and preserve its unique output directory.
2. Run the focused tests and artifact/schema checks named by that phase.
3. Classify each failure as harness, implementation, tuning, numerical,
   candidate, or scientific evidence. Do not call a candidate failure a
   direction failure.
4. Apply the phase repair subplan without changing the target, comparator,
   gates, hardware class, privacy boundary, or total campaign budget.
5. Record command, failure, repair, test result, wall time, and remaining
   budget in the phase repair note.
6. Refresh the next subplan with measured facts, hashes, selected controls,
   and unresolved risks. Mark inherited values as hypotheses unless the phase
   evidence promotes them.
7. Continue automatically to the next phase if its entry gate passes.

A failed candidate blocks that candidate's promotion and triggers its declared
repair. It does not stop later diagnostic work unless a continuation veto fires.

## 8. Real blocker definition

Stop the whole program only for one of these conditions, and record the exact
evidence:

- the target, required data, or source identity is unavailable or contradictory;
- the proposal and target cannot satisfy the required common-support or finite
  integrability condition;
- an exact fixture disproves the mathematical contract and no in-scope repair
  exists;
- three focused repair attempts reproduce the same harness/infrastructure
  failure without meaningful progress;
- the platform blocks an essential operation and no CPU/reference alternative
  answers the current phase; or
- the campaign budget is exhausted.

Poor whitening, low ESS, a missed mode, an unsuccessful arm, or a failed
training screen is not by itself a real blocker. Preserve it, repair it, and
refresh the next subplan.

Phase 6's exact fixture estimated the known mass `2.5` as `2.4812` with MCSE

mutation runs passed hard receipts; their weighted negative occupancy mean was

numbers are descriptive candidate evidence. They do not establish a finite-run
mode-discovery guarantee, an unbiased q=20 normalizer, or authority status.

Phase 24 found a finite structural callback interface but no explicit LEDH
density lifecycle. Phase 25 then measured the induced transition covariance:
its state dimension is `60`, innovation dimension is `20`, and every tested
point has rank `20` (eigenvalue tolerance `1e-10`). The innovation-coordinate
Gaussian density is finite, but the full-state transition is supported on a
lower-dimensional manifold and cannot receive an ordinary 60-dimensional
Lebesgue density. The four-parameter aggregate UKF target is a separate
measure. Direct full-state q=20 LEDH is therefore blocked relative to the
stated target; this is a route-specific measure blocker, not a blocker for
ETPF, SMC, or the broader campaign. Phase 26 tested the smallest reduced-
coordinate fixture before closing the LEDH arm. Phase 26
passed that reduced fixture: the inverse residual was `5.55e-17` and the
density-identity residual was exactly `0` for a positive-definite innovation
covariance. The fixture still has no map from innovation coordinates to the
four-parameter posterior, so direct q=20 LEDH is closed as wrong relative to
the stated target. This is the program's real blocker for that arm. The wider
ETPF/SMC/GenUT/NeuTra evidence remains role-limited and is not invalidated;
HMC remains deferred to a separate reviewed campaign.
