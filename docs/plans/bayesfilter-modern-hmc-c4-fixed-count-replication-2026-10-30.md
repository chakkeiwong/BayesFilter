## Research intent and evidence contract

Question: on fresh streams, how do the original internal stopping screen and
a predeclared fixed retained count differ in estimated precision and independent
reference agreement? Candidate: all seven verified randomized-HMC pairs, in
original order. Expected failure modes are early precision passage with an
unmet reference screen, persistent inadequate precision at the original cap,
or a numerical/initialization failure. The comparator is the earliest prefix
of the same fresh trajectory satisfying the original internal screen, and
the independent frozen covariance-Kalman reference. C3 is historical context,
not a paired random-stream comparator.

The engineering criterion is complete, source-bound accounting for all seven
members and 31 quantities, with exact frozen policies and replayable chunks.
The original posterior screens remain: R-hat <=1.01, bulk/tail ESS >=400,
lugsail MCSE/SD <=.02, and
`(absolute error + 3*MCSE + reference uncertainty)/reference SD <=.1`.
Reference values never determine counts, early stopping, warmup or membership.
No reference-informed retuning occurs. The retained run continues to 10,000
draws per chain regardless of early internal passage, except for health or
resource stops. Warmup still requires the original readiness screen, from
2,000 up to 10,000 discarded draws. A warmup refusal stays in the denominator.

Numerical health failure vetoes that member's assessment and stops its unsafe
continuation; healthy peers continue. Source corruption, wrong seed/policy,
missing required diagnostics or invalid input is a continuation veto requiring
repair. Resource exhaustion preserves chunks for source-matched resume.
Precision/reference failures are posterior promotion vetoes and research repair
triggers, never tuning-membership rejection or automatic direction rejection.
Batch sensitivity, elapsed times, count differences and paired prefix/terminal
differences are explanatory only. One cohort cannot establish calibrated
coverage, superiority, a new default, unseen-tail safety or release readiness.
