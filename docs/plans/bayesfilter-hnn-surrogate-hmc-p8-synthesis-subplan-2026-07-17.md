Phase objective: produce an honest cell-complete result, compare validity and
cost evidence without unsupported rankings, update the monograph, and write a
restartable reset memo.

Entry conditions: P3-P7 results and raw manifests are readable; Tier A is
cell-complete; Tier B is classified.

Required artifacts:

- tried/rejected/untried force-recipe ledger;
- Chapter 48 experimental-results update with exact target labels/nonclaims;

  memo.

Required checks/tests/reviews:

- verify model counts do not count seeds, arms, or filters incorrectly;

  ranking, default readiness, and next evidence;

  HNN validity count;

  accounting, historical status, and omitted failures;

- bounded Claude terminal review of exactly the P8 result path.

Evidence contract: P8 may state which named configurations passed the limited
corrected-kernel test and whether performance was demonstrated under the
recorded evidence. It may not infer calibration, universal reliability, or
superiority without statistical support.

Forbidden claims/actions: no public release, default-policy change, erased
blocker, pooled pass, retroactive threshold change, or model-count inflation.

Exact handoff: reset memo names current code, tests, target/force ledgers,
results, blockers, re-entry rungs, remaining scientific questions, and no
unfinished mandatory phase.

Stop conditions: claim-bearing evidence cannot be reproduced or a material
mathematical/statistical inconsistency remains. Documentation formatting alone
is repairable and does not stop close.

Phase-end duties: run all checks; write terminal result and close record;
perform terminal review; patch material issues and rerun focused checks; issue
the final reset memo.
