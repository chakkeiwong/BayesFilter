# Nonlinear covariance calibration and untouched comparison

This is the next executable slice of the score master, following the main-checkout integration and the observed nonlinear heuristic losses. The question is whether separate calibration of the shared LEDH, SGQF and persistent-mixture covariance proposals yields viable score estimates at N=128, T=4 in each of the already tested weak, curved and concentrated regimes. The model, six-parameter score and independently refined numerical reference remain those specified in `younis-score-nonlinear-continuation-2026-09-15.md`. Increasing N and calibrating controls are a joint repair; comparison with the earlier N=32 pilot cannot isolate their contributions.

The exact comparators are the shared canonical LEDH with UKF covariance, SGQF covariance and persistent-mixture covariance. The constructed heuristic adversaries are EKF (cheap local linearization), UKF (Gaussian moment approximation), bootstrap PF (physical transition with ordinary weighting), and locally linear adapted PF (simple observation-informed importance proposal). All seven are evaluated separately in all three regimes against the same reference and data. A provider is not promoted if it loses to any heuristic. The oracle score error, not ESS, covariance accuracy or the training loss, is the primary scientific quantity.
